Research Protocol

Verification &
Data Integrity

The credibility of this research depends entirely on the integrity of its data set. The following protocol governs how we verify practitioner identity, handle responses, and protect participant privacy — from submission through to publication.

Core Principle: Data is decoupled from identity prior to analysis. We track systemic behavior, not individuals. No individual response is ever attributed to a named person, organization, or identifiable role.
01

Participation & Access

The MOMCI 2026 survey is open to all active and recently active mainframe practitioners. No registration or login is required to participate. Responses are accepted via the survey form and are timestamped at submission. Participants may be invited to a follow-up qualitative interview based on the depth of their written responses — this is entirely voluntary and communicated separately.

02

Credentialing & Token Verification

To maintain a high-integrity data set, responses flagged as outliers — or submitted at anomalous volume from a single origin — are reviewed against technical credentialing signals. Practitioners selected for qualitative interviews receive a unique single-use token that ties their interview record to their survey response without exposing identifying metadata. Tokens expire after 14 days and cannot be reused.

03

Anonymization Pipeline

All raw responses are processed through an anonymization pipeline before they enter the analysis data set. IP addresses, submission timestamps, and any free-text content containing proper nouns (names, organizations, product names) are stripped or generalized. The resulting data set contains only categorical, ordinal, and anonymized narrative data. No re-identification is possible from the published data.

04

Analysis Methodology

Quantitative data is analyzed for distributional patterns, correlation between operational role and toil ratio, and segmentation by team architecture type. Qualitative narrative responses are coded using the Substrata Research methodology — moving from observation to structured pattern recognition while preserving the practitioner's ground-level perspective. Findings are reviewed by the Substrata Research methodology board before publication.

05

Publication & Attribution

Research findings are published to the Technical Library under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. Aggregated statistics may be cited freely with attribution to Mainframe Research / Substrata Research, MOMCI 2026. No individual participant quotes are published without explicit written consent obtained separately from survey participation.

06

Data Retention & Deletion

Raw survey data is retained for 24 months following the close of the collection phase, after which it is permanently deleted. Anonymized analysis data may be retained indefinitely as part of the longitudinal research record. Participants may request deletion of their submission by contacting the research team with their submission date and approximate time — no other identifying information is required or accepted.
